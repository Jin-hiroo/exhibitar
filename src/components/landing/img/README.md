## Example models (who it is for)

The four cards under the audience statement show models that are **not** real exhibits: they
were made for this page as STL files by a small script (revolved, swept and extruded
primitives; each passes the Studio's mesh check with no warnings). Everything in the pictures
after that is the real Studio. Each STL was dropped into `/studio/new` at 1280×1000, 2×, then
Generate model & mat, three points placed with the Point tool and titled in the Annotate tab
(label distance set with its slider where two labels would collide), and the Preview tab
captured at the camera it opens with, cropped square (860 px) around the model and its labels,
2026-10-19. The `-ja` versions come from the same session with the titles retyped in
Japanese. Like `viewer-phone.png`, they were captured with the printed-mat Preview from the
`claude/viewer-printed-mat` work. The page's legend says the models were made for it.
